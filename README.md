## Immersive Skater Exertions for ReSkate

---

A small Luau plugin that plays skate.'s own exertion voice lines when your
skater pops a trick and when they land, with heavier efforts for bigger drops.

The plugin follows the voice you picked in the character creator, so there
is nothing to set up.

Voice support
- Style B ("female"): supported.
- Style A ("male"): coming in a later update. Until then, Style A skaters stay silent.
- None: stays silent, as the game does.

Settings
The defaults should work out of the box. To adjust them, open the console
and run "cg_exert_help". It lists every setting, its range, and why you might
change it.

Permissions
- audio: plays the exertion sounds.
- outfit: reads which voice your skater uses. It never changes your outfit.
- events.skater: hears when you pop and land.
- console: adds the cg_exert_ settings commands.

Good to know
- Only you hear your exertions. Other players hear their own if they have the plugin.
- Made for ReSkate 2.1.4-5 and newer.
